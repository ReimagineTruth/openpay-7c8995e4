## 1. Sign In with Pi (Pi Auth)

- **Slug:** `sign-in-with-pi`
- **Meta:** Authenticate in seconds using your Pi identity — no passwords, no email hoops. Sign in to OpenPay directly from Pi Browser.
- **Tags:** `pi-auth`, `security`, `login`

**Hero:** *One tap. Your Pi identity. Your OpenPay wallet.*

OpenPay uses native Pi authentication so your wallet is bound to the Pioneer you already are. Open OpenPay inside the Pi Browser, tap **Authenticate with Pi**, approve the request, and your session is live. There are no passwords to remember, no OTP delays, and no third-party logins to trust.

Under the hood we verify your Pi access token server-side, mint a short-lived session, and link your Pi UID and username to your OpenPay account so every payment, invoice, and receipt is provably yours.

**CTA:** [Sign in with Pi →](https://openpy.space/auth)
